# NetSense — Success Metrics

## Technical Success (per deployment)
- [ ] Packet loss < 0.1% at 100 Mbps
- [ ] Probe CPU < 30% steady state
- [ ] Discovery precision and recall are measured against a
      customer-agreed validation set, by evidence source and authorised scope
- [ ] > 95% of validated in-scope devices are represented within 1 hour where
      the deployment has sufficient capture or management coverage
- [ ] 100% of displayed operational relationships expose type, provenance,
      observation time, and confidence/limitations
- [ ] Stable entities retain identity across repeated observations and
      address changes in the identity regression suite
- [ ] Zero confirmed-impact claims without configured or observed dependency
      evidence in acceptance tests
- [ ] False positive rate < 20% after 30 days
- [ ] MTTR reduced by > 30% compared to pre-NetSense baseline

## Product Success (across deployments)
- [ ] Setup completion rate > 90%
- [ ] Median time from installation to first useful evidence-backed map < 1
      working day for the supported deployment profile
- [ ] 90% of sampled topology relationships can be explained by an operator
      without development-team assistance
- [ ] Operators identify coverage gaps and stale evidence correctly in > 90%
      of usability test cases
- [ ] 80% of engineers can resolve an alert using dashboard alone (no escalation)
- [ ] Resolution note rate > 50% (engineers document their fixes)
- [ ] Net Promoter Score > 50

## Business Success
- [ ] 10 paying customers by end of Year 1
- [ ] Monthly churn < 5%
- [ ] Customer LTV > 5× CAC
- [ ] Positive cash flow by Month 18
