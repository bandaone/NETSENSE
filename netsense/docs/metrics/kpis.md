# NetSense — Key Performance Indicators

## Product KPIs (tracked once deployed)

| KPI | Definition | Target | Measurement |
|-----|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Time to First Dashboard** | Time from unboxing to seeing topology map | < 20 minutes | Timed setup with new users |
| **Setup Completion Rate** | % of started setups that reach "complete" | > 90% | Wizard analytics |
| **Daily Active Users** | Unique engineers viewing dashboard per day | Per deployment | Login analytics |
| **Alert Accuracy** | True positives / (True positives + False positives) | > 80% after 30 days | Engineer feedback on alerts |
| **Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR)** | Average time from alert to resolution | 50% reduction from pre-NetSense baseline | Incident log |

## Business KPIs (tracked by NetSense company)

| KPI | Definition | Target |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** | Total subscription revenue | Grow 20% month-over-month in first year |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** | Total sales/marketing cost / new customers | < K15,000 |
| **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)** | Average revenue per customer × retention months | > 5× CAC |
| **Churn Rate** | % of customers cancelling per month | < 5% monthly |
| **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** | Survey: "Would you recommend?" | > 50 |
